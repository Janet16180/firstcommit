Sector 8, Time travel. Storyboards, round 5

Five levels: restore, revert, reset, the move log, and the boss. Sector 8 comes after Name tags (5), Branches (6) and Collisions (7). Each level steps beat by beat with Next and Back (or the arrow keys). Every command's output is real git, recorded by .scratch/sector7-design/gen.py. What a player knows: player-knows.md.

How to read the pictures. The chain: every commit once, newest at the top, joined to the one before it. Tags are names: the filled one is the branch you are on, outlined ones are your other branches. HEAD is "you are here": the commit your folder shows; it rides the branch you are on. A dashed tag like origin/main is your note of where the mothership was. Pins show where the mothership (pink) and Alex (green) really are. The desk: your working folder and staging area, with a dashed outline round what Git has a copy of. The move log: git reflog, one row per line it prints, newest at the top: every place HEAD has been. Colours: orange working folder, teal staging area, violet you and your repository, pink mothership, green Alex, gold "look here".
